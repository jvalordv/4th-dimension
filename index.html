How to look at a 4D object

Every point has four coordinates (x, y, z, w). The viewer rotates the object in 4D and shows it in 3D in two honest ways: projection (drop a dimension, the way a photograph does) and slice (the part of the object inside our space w = c). Overlay shows both. Colour is the invisible w: cool is negative, warm is positive.
